# Rechecking module redesign

## Product split

The module has two distinct workspaces selected from a top-level switch:

- **Exam bundle rechecking**: datesheet-driven answer-script chain of custody.
- **Routine book rechecking**: recurring quality sampling for books, notebooks and workbooks.

They do not share a planner, form, table or terminology. They share permissions, notifications, audit history and reporting only.

## Exam bundle rechecking

Choose an exam period first. Show class cards with counts for Unassigned, Assigned, Awaiting recheck, Returned, Completed and Overdue. Opening a class shows a compact paper table:

| Subject | Exam date | Checker | Rechecker | Check due | Recheck due | Status | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Rows enter a deliberate edit mode; they are not permanently white input fields. Saving immediately assigns and notifies staff, then changes the visible status to **Assigned**. The lifecycle drawer is chronological: Assigned → checking/handover → rechecking → completed or returned → repeat round when returned.

## Routine book rechecking

Start with a coverage board, not datesheets. Select class/all accessible classes and show one subject card with timetable checker, last Book/Notebook/Workbook recheck separately, concern level, reviewer note, and days since last recheck.

`Schedule routine review` opens a dedicated panel with fixed class/subject, default checker, required rechecker, material multi-select, sampling week/deadline and optional checker instruction. The review form is material-aware:

| Material | Sample checked | Very poor | Poor | Slightly poor | Incomplete/absent | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Shared UX rules

- Use semantic light/dark tokens only; never hard-code white sticky or editable cells.
- Use card headers, compact rows, sticky headers only, intentional edit mode and mobile card alternatives.
- Planning forms open as a drawer/modal and return to the relevant board after saving.
- Reports have separate Exam/Routine filters: exam focuses deadlines and chain of custody; routine focuses coverage age by material and unresolved issues.

## Core workspace

The Rechecking module has four workspaces:

1. **Exam planner** — choose a datesheet period and assign all relevant paper bundles from a single table.
2. **Routine planner** — choose classes and see subject/material history before scheduling book, notebook, or workbook rechecking.
3. **My actions** — role-specific inbox for checkers and recheckers, grouped by action required.
4. **Quality reports** — status, overdue queues, checker trends, and a class/subject heatmap limited to completed rechecks.

## Exam planner

Selecting a datesheet period loads one row per examination paper:

| Class | Subject | Exam date | Checker | Rechecker | Checking due | Rechecking due | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- The checker is prefilled from the published timetable assignment for that class and subject.
- A manager selects the rechecker and deadlines inline, then saves selected rows or the whole plan.
- Existing duties are shown in place, not duplicated.
- The row opens the lifecycle drawer: checker completion → handover → rechecker review → complete or return → repeat checking round.

## Routine planner

Rows are generated by class and assigned subject, with separate material badges:

| Class | Subject | Book | Notebook | Workbook | Last recheck | Last result | Checker | Rechecker | New duty |

- “Last recheck” and “Last result” come from the most recent completed/returned duty for that exact class and subject.
- Managers can select one or more materials and create duties in bulk.
- Timetable assignment preselects the checker, while a rechecker is deliberately required.

## Permissions

- Admin and superadmin can plan, edit, assign, and report across all classes.
- Teachers can always complete their own checker/rechecker actions.
- A teacher can plan duties only after an admin grants **Rechecking manager** access for the relevant class.
- All permissions remain database-enforced through RLS/RPC, not merely hidden in the UI.

## Notifications and audit

- Assignment: checker and rechecker receive separate queued notices.
- Handover: rechecker receives the total, checking date, and handover time.
- Review: checker receives the quality statistics and notes.
- Return: the original duty advances to a new checking round; prior review data remains immutable.

## Reports

- Operational status: unstarted, awaiting recheck, returned, completed, overdue.
- Checker table: reviewed bundles, very poor, poor, slightly poor, incomplete/absent, returns.
- Heatmap: class × subject concern score, only where rechecking took place.
- Print/PDF-ready report layout uses the school’s existing browser-print workflow.
